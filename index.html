Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Key information regarding turnkey interior fit-outs, authority approvals, and project execution in Dubai.

An end-to-end interior fit-out covers everything required to transform a raw shell-and-core space into a fully functional environment. At Libra Interiors, our turnkey solutions include design planning, structural modifications, gypsum or glass partition walls, premium flooring installation, bespoke joinery, technical lighting setups, and final furniture implementation.

Yes, most commercial interior fit-outs and structural changes in Dubai require official approvals from local authorities such as Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, TECOM, or Concordia, depending on your building’s location. A professional interior contracting company handles the technical drawings, documentation, and approval process to ensure complete regulatory compliance.

Glass and acoustic partition systems are highly efficient for optimizing office space without sacrificing natural light. Glass partitions create an open, modern corporate aesthetic, while acoustic partition walls provide necessary sound insulation for private meeting rooms and executive offices, balancing collaboration with privacy.

Absolutely. Our in-house joinery and carpentry teams specialize in crafting bespoke wooden elements tailored to your brand identity. We design, manufacture, and install custom reception desks, wall paneling, built-in wardrobes, retail display units, and unique storage setups using premium materials and precision craftsmanship.

For high-traffic commercial environments, durability and maintenance are key. We highly recommend heavy-duty vinyl flooring, premium carpet tiles, or industrial epoxy flooring for modern office spaces and retail stores. For luxury villas or executive boardrooms, classic marble, granite, or wooden laminate flooring offers a timeless, sophisticated finish.
